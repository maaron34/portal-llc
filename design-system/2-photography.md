# Photography

Every photo shows a real Portal job or the Portal crew. People hire Portal on the strength of the work in the pictures, so the pictures have to be the work they will get. Chris reviews them.

## Rules

- Use only Portal's own jobs and crew. Never use stock photos, another contractor's work, or images made or altered by AI.
- Show the finished concrete clearly: daylight, the whole surface in frame, edges and step noses visible.
- Shoot before and after pairs from the same spot at the same angle. Show BEFORE first or on the left.
- Show the crew working: demo, forms, pour, finish. Hi-vis gear and a messy site are fine.
- Edit lightly: straighten, crop, exposure and white balance only. Never remove cracks, retouch the concrete or replace the sky.
- Crop so the concrete stays the subject. Set the crop point (`object-position`) so steps and edges stay in frame.
- If no real photo fits, change the layout or ask Chris for a new photo. Never fill the gap with stock.
- Do not use the "stairs 3" before/after pair. The after photo looks like painted steps.
- When photos from different jobs appear in one sequence, label them as steps ("Demo." "Pour." "Finish.") and never as one continuous project.

## Text on photos

- In ads and video, put text in a `portal-scrim` box with white type.
- On the website, put tile titles on a bottom gradient that runs from `portal-dark` at 80% to clear.
- Keep text off faces and off the concrete you are trying to show.
