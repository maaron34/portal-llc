# Banned patterns

These patterns make work look generic or machine-made. People notice them even when they cannot name them, and they undercut a brand built on real crews and real jobs. None of them appear in anything Portal publishes.

## Layout

- Eyebrows: a small uppercase or letter-spaced label above a heading, such as "OUR SERVICES" over "What we do". The heading does the job on its own.
- Numbered section markers (01, 02, 03) on content that is not a real sequence of steps.
- A tinted square with an icon in it above every card title, repeated three across a row.
- Colored bars or borders down the left side of cards.
- Drop shadows, glows, blurred glass panels and floating blobs.
- Full-screen hero gradients with a slogan in the middle.
- Rows of big-number tiles ("500+ projects") without a source.
- Every block wrapped in the same rounded card.

## Color and type

- Gradients of any kind, except the bottom gradient on a ServiceTile and the 200px fade above the photo band on a video end card.
- Blue, purple, neon or a second accent color.
- A second typeface added for personality, or a font that imitates the logo.
- All-caps labels with wide letter spacing anywhere except the BEFORE and AFTER tags.
- One word alone on the last line of a heading, caption or button, and two-line text with lopsided lines. Break lines by hand so both lines carry a similar number of words. This applies to pages, ads, videos and emails.

## Images

- Stock photos, AI-generated images, illustrated workers, 3D renders and isometric houses.
- Emoji used as icons or bullets.
- Mockups of phones or laptops around a photo of concrete.

## Copy

- Sentences built as "X, not Y" or "It's not just X, it's Y".
- Abstract triplets such as "Quality. Integrity. Excellence."
- Filler words: seamless, unlock, transform, journey, game-changer, next level.
- A colon followed by a reveal ("The secret: preparation").
- Em dashes.
- Questions used as every section heading.
