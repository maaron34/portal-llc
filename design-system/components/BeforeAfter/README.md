Two photos of the same spot, before and after the job, each tagged in its top-left corner.

## Use it for
- Repair, replacement and reconditioning work, on the website, in ads and in video.

## The consumer provides
- Two real photos of one Portal job, shot from the same spot at the same angle.
- Alt text that says what each photo shows.

## Rules
- BEFORE comes first or on the left, tagged on `portal-scrim`. AFTER is tagged on `accent-fill`.
- Tags use `ad-tag` sizing in ads (26px at 1200 square) and `video-tag` in video. They are the only all-caps text in the system.
- Split the photos with a 6px `portal-dark` gap in ads. On the website, a `space-3` gap is fine.
- Never pair photos from two different jobs, and never retouch the after photo.
