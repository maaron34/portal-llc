The closing card of every video: white logo, headline, rating, Free estimate badge and URL on charcoal, centered in the safe zone.

## Use it for
- The last 3.4 seconds of every Reel, Short and Story, the first 0.4 of them shared with the transition in.

## The consumer provides
- A headline that sums up the video, one or two proof lines from Voice and claims, and one finished-job photo for the bottom band.

## Rules
- Use `portal-charcoal` for the ground. Stack 38px apart: logo at 460px wide, `video-endcard-head`, `video-endcard-line` in `portal-light`, CtaBadge in `video-cta`, URL in `video-url` and `portal-warm`.
- Five `portal-star` stars sit directly above the Google rating line.
- Center the stack between y 270 and y 1248, with 150px clear on both sides.
- Fill y 1290 to 1920 with the job photo under a 200px charcoal fade. Keep text and the logo out of it.
- This is the only place the URL appears in video or ads.
