A one-line customer quote set in italics, centered, with the author in green.

## Use it for
- Between a section heading and its content, or below a hero, when a short review backs up the point.

## The consumer provides
- A short quote from the approved list in Voice and claims, and its author.

## Rules
- Quote in `ink-muted`, italic, 16px. Author upright in `accent-ink`, weight 500, after a spaced hyphen.
- One pull quote per section at most. Never put it above a heading as a label.
