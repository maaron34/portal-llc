A customer review on a white card: five stars, the quote in italics, and the reviewer's first name and last initial.

## Use it for
- Rows of three reviews on a `surface-alt` band, on the home page, landing pages and the reviews page.

## The consumer provides
- The review text word for word, and the author as first name plus last initial.

## Rules
- Card is `surface` with `radius-lg` and `space-8` padding, no border and no shadow.
- Quote in `ink-soft`, italic, 16px on 26px. Author in `ink`, 14px, 600.
- Trim reviews only from the start or the end.
