The green pill that says "Free estimate" in static ads and on the video end card.

## Use it for
- The single call to action in every static ad and every video end card.

## The consumer provides
- Nothing but the size: `ad-cta` (44px at 1200 square, 42px portrait, 30px wide) or `video-cta` (64px).

## Rules
- `accent-fill` with `on-accent` text in 900 weight, `radius-pill`, padding 0.6em by 1.15em (26px by 60px in video).
- The text is always "Free estimate". Do not swap in "Call now", "Learn more" or a phone number.
- It is a label, because the whole ad is the link. Never use it as a website button; use Button there.
- One per ad. Never pair it with a second call to action.
