A video scene: a full-bleed job photo with a caption box, and a BEFORE or AFTER tag when the scene is part of a pair.

## Use it for
- Every photo scene in a 1080 x 1920 Reel, Short or Story.

## The consumer provides
- A real job photo, a caption of two lines at most, and a tag if the scene is half of a before/after pair.

## Rules
- Caption: `video-caption`, white on `portal-scrim`, `radius-caption`, top-left of the safe zone.
- Tag: `video-tag`, above the caption, 22px apart.
- Keep everything inside the safe zone (`video-safe-top`, `video-safe-bottom`, `video-safe-left`, `video-safe-right`).
- Fade the caption in over `duration-caption-fade`, 0.25 seconds into the scene, while the photo zooms 8%.
